What is an Energy Performance Certificate?

An EPC (Energy Performance Certificate ) is a legal document required when a property is built, sold or rented.

The certificate will outline how energy efficient a building is and give it a rating from A (very efficient) to G (inefficient). This is shown in the form of a coloured bar chart, similar to those you find on newer electrical appliances.

This rating will take into account the energy performance of the property itself (the fabric) and its services (heating, lighting, hot water etc).

In addition to this, the EPC document will also show:

  • a breakdown of a property’s energy performance
  • its environmental impact
  • recommendations for improvements
  • estimated energy use alongside potential savings
