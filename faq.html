Frequently asked questions

To produce the certificate, what aspects does the assessor look at?

During the assessment, the assessor examines the following aspects:

  • Loft insulation: The assessor inspects the loft space to record insulation depth and type, usually by taking photographs with a tape measure.
  • Wall type/thickness: The assessor measures the volume of the property and calculates heat loss perimeter based on wall type and insulation.
  • Heating/hot water system: The assessor notes the type of heating system, boiler make/model, and any heating controls present.
  • Windows: The assessor checks window type and age.
  • Lighting: The assessor counts the number of lights and assesses if they are energy-efficient.

What evidence does the assessor provide to support their findings?

The assessor provides evidence in two forms:

  • Photographic evidence: Pictures taken during the assessment day.
  • Documentary evidence: Documentation may be required for aspects like wall or loft insulation that cannot be seen during the assessment.
